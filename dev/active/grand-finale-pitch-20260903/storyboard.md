# BidPilot Grand Finale Storyboard

This storyboard separates on-screen copy, visual evidence, narration purpose, and timing for a ten-slide finale deck.

**Contents**: 1 Narrative contract · 2 Slide plan · 3 Demonstration insertion · 4 Visual system · 5 Change history

## 1 Narrative contract

1. The presentation opens with Sergio Lee’s field discipline and connects it immediately to the pursuit problem.
2. The product is introduced as a decision and execution workspace, not as a summarizer.
3. The live demonstration separates the real public tender from the synthetic historical replay.
4. Evidence is placed beside the relevant official evaluation criterion.
5. The close reserves the final twenty seconds for “Win the score, not the prompt.”

## 4 Visual system

1. Format is 16:9 with a near-black canvas and one cool blue accent.
2. Headings use large Apple Keynote-like editorial scale with restrained weight and negative tracking.
3. Each slide carries one claim and no decorative illustration.
4. Product screenshots are the only dense surfaces and appear as large, sharply cropped evidence.
5. Rubric labels appear only where evidence is shown: `30% Relevance`, `40% Technical Execution`, and `30% Completeness`.
6. Em dash characters, synthetic people imagery, dense card grids, gradients, glows, and decorative AI motifs are excluded.
