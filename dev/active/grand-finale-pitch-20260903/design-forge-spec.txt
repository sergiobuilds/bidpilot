Build a self-contained 12-slide horizontal presentation for the Snowflake CoCo CLI Hackathon 2026 Grand Finale.

The presentation is a calm professional data narrative. Every slide is led by one central SVG graph, diagram, table, geometric composition, or process visualization. Text and numbers label the visual. They are never the visual by themselves.

Global functional requirements:
- Produce exactly 12 slides in one HTML file named index.html.
- Use a fixed 1440 by 810 design canvas and scale it to fit the viewport.
- Support ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, PageUp, PageDown, Home, End, Space, and direct hash navigation from #1 through #12.
- Keep every slide inside one viewport with no document scroll.
- Use wanted-design-system tokens, type classes, semantic colors, and icons only.
- Use Wanted Sans Variable as the visible typeface.
- Keep the canvas calm and light. This is a data and operations presentation.
- Make each slide title an English noun phrase separated by middle dots. Do not use question titles.
- Keep titles concise and place them close to the central visual rather than in a repeated presentation header.
- Do not add a repeated logo bar, progress bar, navigation chrome, rubric badge, or dashboard shell.
- Show only a small page number in the lower-right corner.
- Keep ordinary visible text at 24 CSS pixels or larger on the 1440 by 810 canvas. Source notes may be 16 to 18 pixels.
- Keep on-screen prose below 35 words per slide, excluding table labels and source notes.
- Use one dominant visual per slide, centered on the canvas with generous negative space.
- Use SVG for charts, flows, score bars, gates, connectors, curves, evidence nodes, and diagrams.
- Use flat WDS semantic colors and precise hairlines. Avoid decorative atmosphere that competes with data.
- Use WDS primary blue only for verified, selected, or active paths.
- Use WDS caution color only for REVIEW and unresolved evidence.
- Use cool-neutral gray for inactive, alternative, or contextual paths.
- Use actual product screenshots only as small authenticity insets on slides 7 and 8. Do not make a screenshot the main composition.
- Keep all slide copy as HTML text. SVG labels may remain SVG text if needed.
- Add accessible labels to diagrams and meaningful alt text to screenshots.
- Use quiet 200 to 300 millisecond transitions and support prefers-reduced-motion.

Hard anti-pattern rules:
- No row of three or four equal number cards.
- No KPI tiles.
- No repeated cards with icon, title, and body.
- No generic dashboard grids.
- No sequence made only from rounded rectangles.
- No giant standalone number without a meaningful scale, relationship, or graph.
- No decorative AI network, neural nodes, sparkles, floating blobs, glass panels, or generated imagery.
- No stock illustration or human portrait.
- No em dash.
- No “not just X, but Y”, “more than X”, or “revolutionize”.
